# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 15 July 2026**

Video Compressor ("the app") is developed by [Fybre](https://fybre.me). This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

## Summary

Video Compressor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All video processing happens entirely on your device.

## Data Collection

The app does not collect any data. Specifically:

- **No analytics or tracking** — the app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks of any kind.
- **No network access** — the app does not make any network requests. Your videos never leave your device.
- **No account or sign-in** — the app does not require or support any form of account creation or sign-in.
- **No third-party services** — the app does not integrate with any third-party SDKs or services.

## Permissions

The app requests the following iOS permissions, used only for the stated purpose:

- **Photo Library (select)** — to let you choose a video to compress.
- **Photo Library (add)** — to let you save a compressed video back to your photo library, if you choose to.

Video files you select or save are processed locally and are never uploaded anywhere.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.

Questions about this policy can be directed via [fybre.me](https://fybre.me) or by opening an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Fybre/VideoCompressor).
